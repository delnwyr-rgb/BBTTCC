## Two findings the lint added after the first cut

- **OP deltas are in MARKS, not OP (E02, 31 rows, 28 in Act 2).** `factionEffects[].opDeltas`
  goes straight to `op.commit`, which counts marks (1 OP = 10 marks). Every authored value is
  1, 2, −1 or −7 — i.e. 0.1–0.7 OP. If those were meant as whole OP, author them ×10.
- **`api.factions.applyDelta` is not defined anywhere in the repo source**, and the world-mutation
  engine gates every factionEffects row on it. The walk (below) showed it DOES exist at runtime on
  ember — meters moved (morale, loyalty, victory.unity, darkness.global) — so faction effects land.
  Which module attaches it is still unknown to grep; worth a note in the atlas, not an alarm.

## ERRORS (fix before play)

1. **`op.cult` is not an OP channel** — `balcones_faulting_you_line_opening` + `_choices`
   choice "Impose your will", `gilbert_theater_parley` choice "Appeal to culture and civic joy"
   (Act 3). The enact path splits `op.<key>` and reads the faction bank at `opBank.cult`, which
   nobody has: the choice is refused "cannot pay", or under desperation commits −10 marks into a
   phantom `cult` channel. **Fix: `checkStat: "op.culture"`.** (The label map knows `op.cult`,
   which is why it *looks* fine in the editor.)
2. **`vs_overture` gate `{questBucket: Khezek Tor, isNot: "unstarted"}`** — the engine's buckets
   are `active | completed | archived`; an unknown bucket is *unmet forever*, so the Valhaulan
   Spine overture can never be offered by the director. Fix: express "not started" as the two
   conditions `{questBucket, isNot:"active"}` + `{questBucket, isNot:"completed"}` (AND), or
   drop the condition if the turn gate is enough.
3. **`travel_ashwastes_t1` has zero entries** (as of the 08-24 tables) — `runRandomTable` warns
   and fires nothing. Re-check against a fresh bundle; seed it if still empty.

## Layer 2 — the headless walker (built 2026-09-05, owner-run)

`modules/bbttcc-campaign/tools/walk-act-headless.macro.js` — GM macro, deployed to both live
instances. Run it as GM on ember with "Thatward's Ho!" active and **no players connected**
(≈5–8 min for Act 2; console shows progress; a summary card + `walk-act2-<date>.json` download
land at the end). `CFG` at the top: `ACT`, `ONLY` (regex/ids), `LIMIT`, `FAIL_PATHS`, `KEEP_CHAT`.
Emergency stop: `window.__bbttccWalkStop = true` in the console.

What it does, per beat, through the REAL pipeline in a throwaway campaign with temp factions:

1. **Effects pass** — fires the beat with no choice taken; asserts it resolved, its questEffects
   landed in the faction's quest buckets, phaseAdvance/tikkunDelta moved the meters, and its
   factionEffects opDeltas/morale landed (or flags the ENGINE GAP above).
2. **Transition pass** — enacts every labeled choice exactly as the dialogue engine does; the
   routed beat's dialog is auto-closed so the chain stops after one hop; asserts routed ===
   `next`. Checked choices run twice: forced PASS (d20 pinned to 20 / GM adjudication SUCCESS)
   and forced FAIL (d20 = 1 / FAIL) → asserts `next` / `failNext`, and the −10-mark debit on OP
   checks.

What it deliberately does NOT touch: live hexes (purifyHexes/radiation/territoryOutcome/
gateUnlocks stripped, recorded as declared), real factions (foreign factionIds remapped to temp
actors), scenes (activate/view stubbed), encounters (launchers stubbed), the turn queue, time
(addTime stubbed), NPC memories (snapshotted/restored), chat (deleted after). Everything else
— settings, active campaign, director state — is snapshotted and restored in `finally`.
