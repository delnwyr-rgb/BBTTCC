# BBTTCC Ancestry Canon — v2.0

*— Mal speaking —*

Alright. We cut the ancestry list. Not because the old one was wrong, but because eight is how many names you can keep in your head when a faction meeting starts going sideways. Here's what the world is now.

---

## The Eight

**Cryptidkin.** The rumors that walked off the page. Something the farmers whisper about, or the kids draw on the inside of a locker, or the old radio operators swear they saw near the ley gate. You're a story that the world decided to make people out of. Pick your flavor: the thing that drains the goats (*Chupacabra*), the omen that runs faster than the news (*Jackalope*), or the fur-bearing cousin that finally stopped pretending to be an animal (*Furrykin* — badger to Bigfoot, you decide, it's one stat block).

**Echo-Diver.** The lions are gone. The wolves are gone. The kings are gone. You're still here. That's not an accident. You always knew which doorway was going to collapse a half-second before it did, and in a world made of collapsing doorways, that half-second made you the inheritor. The rats of the new world. Say it with your chest. A rat outlives a lion.

**Menhirkin.** You are not *from* a place. You *are* a place. Someone asked the bedrock what it thought, and the bedrock stood up and started answering. When you walk into a hex, the hex recognizes you. When you leave, it feels it. You're what the ley-grid looks like when it decides to have opinions.

**Oldenborn.** The old gods didn't die. They just got smaller. You are a place-soul that learned to wear legs, and the land still whispers your real name when no one's listening. Four ways to fall out of Yesod: rooted in the *Earthbound* dirt, lit up *Lumenwrought* from inside, riding the ash squalls as a *Stormborn Nomad*, or scavenging the bones of dead cities as a *Rustland Scavenger*.

**Human.** Not a fantasy. The rebooted inheritance of an older lineage that refused to stay extinct. Four branches: *Neanderthal* kept the strength, *Florensis* kept the reach, *Cro-Magnon* kept the pattern — the language, the ritual, the stubborn coalition instinct that built the first thing worth defending. And *Denisovan* kept the altitude — high-blood mountain-memory, stone-singing, gravity-listening, the quiet cousin who was always the one that knew what the rocks were saying.

**Qliph-Scarred.** You walked out of something that should have unmade you, and the hollows in your soul never fully closed. The world can feel the pressure leaking out of you. People cross the street. You don't mind. The distance helps you think.

**Sephirotic Scion.** Your blood remembers the higher spheres. Light hangs around you a little too long. Prayers sometimes answer themselves. You experience the world's moral architecture the way other people experience the weather.

**Circuitborn.** You are not a robot. You are a decision the world keeps making about itself. The networks are alive and they recognize you on sight. Four frames: the war-plate *Exo-Knight*, the paradox-walking *Parallax*, the salvage-recursive *Salvage*, or the ghost-in-the-protocol *Synapse*.

---

## What got cut

- **Sky-Threaded.** The Oldenborn who claimed the sky. The sky claimed them back.
- **Ember-Touched.** Too narrow. The fire-adjacent fiction is covered by Lumenwrought and Rustland now.
- **Erectus.** Replaced by Cro-Magnon. Don't mourn him. He's still in the genome.
- **The five separate Furrykin clades** (Felid / Leporid / Mustelid / Ursid / Vulpin). Now one heritage. If you want to be a fox, be a fox. If you want to be a bear, be a bear. The stat block doesn't care.

And two species got demoted into Oldenborn heritages, because they were always Oldenborn wearing a different hat:

- **Stormborn Nomad** → Oldenborn heritage
- **Rustland Scavenger** → Oldenborn heritage *(also picked up an 'r' — it's Rustland now, not Rustlander)*

---

## For the steward sheet

When the wizard asks for your species, pick one of the eight. When it asks for your subspecies or heritage, pick from that family's list. If you used to be something that isn't on the list anymore, the GM will tell you what the land remembers you as now.

The paperwork is cleaner. The fiction is the same.

That's the whole pitch.
